# Privacy Policy for Off-Beat

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Off-Beat mobile game ("the App") handles your information.

## Information We Collect

Off-Beat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

The App works entirely offline. Any game data, such as scores, progress, or settings, is stored only on your device and never leaves it. We have no access to this data.

## Permissions

Off-Beat does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other personal data on your device.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or account sign-in.

## Children's Privacy

Because Off-Beat does not collect any information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Deleting Your Data

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

## Changes to This Policy

If future versions of Off-Beat add features that collect or share data, this policy will be updated before those changes are released, and the effective date above will be revised.
